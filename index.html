Welcome!

This is a submission for the project "Revisiting Rock Paper Scissors" in the Foundations path of The Odin Project.

Now, the project only asks to add a couple buttons and a div for displaying the result. While that was a nice task, I felt like I can't apply my favourite motto:

"If life is too easy, overcomplicate it!"

So, since I have a little experience in Godot, I naturally thought I would make it into a bit more interactive game. I still wanted to keep it relatively simple though, and this is what I settled on. The controls are easy: WASD to move, Space to restart after the game is over. What more could I need?

However, I still wanted to include my original assignment too (what the lesson actually asked for). So here's both! Choose whichever you want.
